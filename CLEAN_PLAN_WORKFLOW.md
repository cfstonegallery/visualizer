# Clean Plan Workflow

Use this workflow when Chrissy supplies a faint, marked-up, scanned, photographed, or hard-to-read room plan and wants a clean overhead image.

## Why this step exists

A clean plan makes the room shape, cabinets, fixtures, furniture, openings, and readable measurements easier to understand. It can become the checked starting point for later room views and material visualizations.

A clean plan is a planning picture. It is not automatically a construction, ordering, or installation drawing.

## Step 1: Keep the original

- Confirm the active job folder.
- Save an unchanged copy of the source inside that job's `floor-plans` folder.
- Never replace the source plan with the cleaned image.

## Step 2: Read before redrawing

List what can be seen with confidence:

- Room outline and wall angles.
- Doors, windows, and open passages.
- Cabinets, counters, islands, and appliances.
- Plumbing fixtures and other fixed items.
- Furniture that explains how the room is used.
- Measurements that are clearly readable.
- Marks or words that are too faint or unclear to trust.

Do not guess at an unreadable measurement or hidden cabinet detail.

## Step 3: Confirm what must remain

Before removing items as clutter, confirm whether they are part of the useful layout. This includes tables, chairs, benches, islands, fixtures, and other furniture.

If the answer is already clear from Chrissy's request, use it. If it is unclear and would change the result, ask one short question.

## Step 4: Make the clean draft

- Use a white background and clear dark outlines.
- Use simple shading or color to separate cabinets or other requested areas.
- Preserve the source room shape and item positions.
- Show only measurements that can be read or that Chrissy confirms.
- Mark any uncertain detail in the review notes instead of inventing it.
- Keep the image easy to compare with the original.

## Step 5: Review beside the original

Check these items with Chrissy:

- Room shape and wall angles.
- Doors, windows, and openings.
- Cabinet, counter, island, and appliance positions.
- Fixture count and placement.
- Furniture that should remain.
- Every displayed measurement.
- Anything omitted because it could not be read.

The draft is not an approved source until Chrissy confirms the layout and measurements.

## Step 6: Save only after approval

Ask whether the exact draft is worth saving and what filename Chrissy wants. Do not overwrite another image unless she clearly asks.

Keep the handoff connected to both the unchanged source and the approved cleaned image.

## Step 7: Use it carefully

After Chrissy approves it, the clean plan may guide:

- Room views.
- Material placement.
- Tile direction and starting-point discussions.
- Customer explanations.
- Proposals and internal handoffs.

Do not use it by itself to order materials, set installation measurements, or direct construction unless the measurements and details have been checked against reliable field or architectural information.
